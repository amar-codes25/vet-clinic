In-House Laboratory & Diagnostic Testing

Our in-house lab allows us to perform a wide range of diagnostic tests quickly and accurately, often providing results during your pet's visit.

Tests We Offer

Blood Work

Comprehensive blood chemistry panels and Complete Blood Counts (CBCs) to assess organ function and overall health.

Urinalysis & Fecal Exams

Essential for detecting underlying health issues, infections, and parasites.

Cytology

Microscopic examination of cells to evaluate lumps, skin conditions, and ear infections.

Digital Radiography (X-rays) & Ultrasound

Advanced imaging to help diagnose conditions affecting bones, organs, and soft tissues.

Good to Know

  • Most tests can be done during a scheduled appointment.
  • Some tests may require fasting — please call ahead.
  • Results are often available the same day.
